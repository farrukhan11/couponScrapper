## Helpful Information About Ivory and Co

About Ivory and Co

Ivory and Co is a prominent British luxury brand specializing in award-winning bridal accessories. Established in Worcestershire, the company offers an extensive range of items, including tiaras, hairpieces, bridal jewelry, bridal belts, hair clips, and hair combs, with over 890 unique products available. Each piece is ethically sourced, handcrafted, and designed in-house, aiming to create timeless heirlooms for special occasions. Ivory and Co also features a flagship showroom in Upton-upon-Severn, which provides bespoke styling sessions for brides by appointment. In addition to their direct-to-consumer website, they supply bridal boutiques and department stores across Europe, reinforcing their status as a global brand in the bridal market.
