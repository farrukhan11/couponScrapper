Explore this section to find comprehensive answers to all your questions about the loyalty program.

Why haven’t my points been added to my account immediately?

Your points will be credited to your account after your order has been shipped.

Do my points expire?

Currently, we do not set an expiration date for your points. Your points will remain valid permanently.

Can I use point redemption discount coupons together with promotional offers?

We’re sorry, but discount coupons redeemed with points cannot be combined with other promotional offers or discounts.

My points balance seems incorrect. What should I do?

Please contact our service team at service@kailashenergy.com. Our team will assist you in checking and verifying your points balance.

Do I need to register to become a member? Is membership free?

Yes. You need to register with your email address, and joining our Membership program is completely free.

Can I earn points during promotional periods?

Absolutely. You can earn points at any time, including during promotional periods.

What happens to my points if I cancel my order?

If an order is canceled or refunded, the points earned from that order will be deducted from your account.

Will I lose my points if I change my email address?

Your points are linked to your membership account email address. If you need to change your email address, please contact our customer service team for assistance.
