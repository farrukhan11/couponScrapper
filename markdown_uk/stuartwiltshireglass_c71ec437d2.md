### About the company

Based in Weymouth, England, Stuart Wiltshire Glass turns hand-blown ashes into glass keepsakes and jewelry. It was all started in 2009 by Stuart Wiltshire, a glass artist having more than 27 years of experience. Stuart moved to Sweden to work for Orrefors. It is home of glass blowing. His passion is to create long lasting memories for families through artwork. Today, Stuart Wiltshire Glass become the 2nd most popular destination in Weymouth.

Why choose Stuart Glasses over others?

* Received 4.5 out of 5 stars from over 403 reviews at TrustPilot.
* Cremation ashes memorials in hearts, paperweights, tea light holders, and pebbles.
* They return any unused ashes with the finished memorial piece.
* Utilized the highest Quality Barium Glass.
* Each order is hand blown in 5 to 10 Days with full satisfaction guarantee.

### Transparent pricing structure and services

Stuart Wiltshire Glass maintains accessible pricing for premium artisan work:

1. **Mini ashes memorials**: It starts from £80 (options are; heart, pebble, oval paperweight)
2. **Standard memorials:** £80 to £200+ (tea light holders, paperweights, sculptures)
3. **Ashes Jewellery:** Costs between £70 to £300 (necklace, cat, dog and pet)
