Order & Shipping

How long will it take to receive my order?

Once we received your order, it will be on it's way to you within **1 business day**even if it is personalized.

Once dispatched, you'll receive a tracking code and transit times are the following:

**Spain:** 24–48 hours

**Europe & UK:** 2–5 business days

**Rest of the world:** 5–10 business days

How to track my order?

Please track your order at our Tracking page with the tracking number you have received by email : https://emporizen.com/tools/track-order

How much is shipping?

Shipping is 100% free, no matter where you are.

I never got my order, what to do?

Please make sure you tracked your order from our Tracking page: https://emporizen.com/tools/track-order

If you still need help, please contact us here: https://emporizen.com/pages/contact-us

Exchange & Return

What is your return policy?

We have a **30-day return policy**, which means you have 30 days after receiving your item to request a return.

To start a return, you can contact us at contact@emporizen.com

For additional info, please check our return/refund/exchange policy here.

Can I exchange an item?

Yes, the fastest way to ensure you get what you want is to return the item you have, and once the return is accepted, make a separate purchase for the new item. See detailed policy here.

Can I return a personalized item?

Unfortunately, we don’t accept returns on personalized items unless they arrive damaged or with a mistake.

How do I request an exchange or return?

Just email us at **contact@emporizen.com** with your order number and reason. We'll guide you through the process.

Products & Customization

Can I adjust the watch to my wrist?

Yes. Our wooden watches come with instructions and a link removal tool to easily adjust the strap size by yourself.

Can my watch be engraved?

Yes! Our watches offercustom engraving on the backplate, perfect for gifts or special moments.

Just:

1. Go to the watch you want to personalize
2. Press the button **“Add Engraving”**
3. Enter your text
4. Select your font (e.g. Open Sans)
5. Add to cart & checkout

What type of wood do you use?

We use a variety of natural woods like bamboo, ebony, sandalwood, walnut and olive wood, always sustainably sourced.

Do you offer a warranty?

Yes we do, all our watches come with a 2-year warranty covering manufacturing defects. If you experience any issues, just reach out and we’ll be happy to help.

Are Emporizen watches water proof?

Our watches are **splashproof**but not waterproof, so a little rain won't harm them.

Please avoid wearing them while swimming or showering, as the warranty does not cover water damage.

How do I keep my wood watch in perfect condition?

* Clean it regularly with a soft, dry cloth to remove any dust or moisture.

* Avoid exposure to water, extreme temperatures and harsh chemicals.

* Occasionally, apply a small amount of natural oil, to keep the wood conditioned and prevent it from drying out.

What type of metal is used for the clasp of the watch?

The type of metal used for the clasp of your Emporizen watch is stainless steel.

Do you offer corporate customization and pricing?

Yes! We offer special pricing for bulk orders and custom engraving for corporate gifts, including your company logo, a message, or design on the backplate.

Interested? Contact us for a personalized quote.

Payments & Security

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept credit/debit cards, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Shop Pay, Klarna, and other secure local payment options.,

Is it safe to order from Emporizen?

Absolutely. Our website uses encrypted and secure payment gateways to keep your data protected.

Is VAT included in the prices shown?

Yes, all prices on our website include VAT where applicable.

For orders shipped outside the EU, local import duties, taxes, or customs fees may apply. These are the buyer's responsibility. If you're unsure about potential charges, we recommend contacting your local customs office.

Sustainability & Mission

What makes Emporizen sustainable?

Our accessories are mainly made from FSC-Certified wood, and our packaging is crafted of 100% natural bamboo, minimizing the use of plastic plastic.

In addition, we plant 1 tree for ervery item you purchase through our partnership with Tree-Nation and we partner with environmentally conscious suppliers.

Do you plant 1 tree with every order?

Yes! 🌱 For every item sold, we plant 1 tree through our partnership with Tree Nation. You'll receive a personal link with the certificate and exact location of your planted trees.

Where are the trees planted?

Trees are planted in reforestation projects across Africa, including Kenya, Madagascar, Tanzania... Your planting certificate will show the exact project.
