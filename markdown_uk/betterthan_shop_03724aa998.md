### What Audis and Lamborghinis is the bTres compatible with?

The bTres is compatible with any DS1 tuned car. These are generally Audi RS3, RSQ3, S6, RS6, S7, RS7, S8, A8, R8s. If you have a different audi with one of the mentioned audi's engines swapped in, it will also work. Along with Audi's it will also work with the Lamborghini Huracan.

### Does the bTres require the car to be tuned with DS1 and is the DS1 required to be plugged in?

Yes, the tune must be an OTS DS1 tune or custom DS1 tune and the DS1 dongle must be plugged in for the bTres to communicate in real-time with.

### Can I remove the bTres and sell it if I no longer want it or sell my car?

If you buy the locked version, the bTres will marry to your car's VIN and cannot be used on another car afterwards. Some exceptions can be made on a case by case basis. Feel free to contact us if you have any questions or need in unmarried.

If you purchase the unlocked version, it will not marry to any VIN and can be swapped to other cars.
