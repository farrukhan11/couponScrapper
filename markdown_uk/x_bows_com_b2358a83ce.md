Detailed X-Bows Crystal review

I picked up the X-Bows Crystal with Blue Clicky Switches, plus an extra Custom Coiled Cable and a Felt Keyboard Carrying Case. Ordering was simple enough through their website, though I had a quick question about the PBT keycaps which their support team answered within a day – pretty responsive. The keyboard arrived well-packaged; it took 6 days to reach my address in Texas. Setting it up was plug-and-play. The radial layout is definitely unique, and it took me a good few days to adjust, especially to the split spacebar. The RGB backlighting is bright and has several patterns, and programming macros with their software was fairly intuitive for basic commands. The PBT keycaps feel premium, much better than standard ABS. The Custom Coiled Cable added a nice touch to my desk setup, and the Carrying Case is practical for when I need to take it to the office. My only minor complaint would be that the wrist rest, while included, could be a bit softer for extended use. Still, a very solid keyboard and overall experience.
