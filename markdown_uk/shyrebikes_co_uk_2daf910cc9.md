## Shyre Bikes affiliate FAQ

How long does the approval process take?

Fill out the sign-up form in a few minutes and we’ll review your application within our next working days — usually within 1 working day.


How will I know if my application is approved?

Our team carefully reviews every application. If yours is approved, you’ll receive an email confirming your acceptance and some promotion assets to help you get started. If it’s not, we’ll email you too.


How are referrals tracked?

On acceptance you get your own affiliate dashboard to manage your links and coupons. When customers buy using your link or coupon, the sale is credited to you. Even if they don’t order on the first visit, your affiliate cookie is stored for 90 days — so you still earn if they come back and buy before it expires.


How and when do I get paid?

Affiliates get paid directly to the payment account of their choice, or as discounts converted from store credit. Payouts are made on the basis both parties agree — after each referral, when your balance reaches a set amount, or on a weekly/monthly schedule.


Is there a cost to join?

No — there’s no joining fee. It’s completely free to become a Shyre affiliate and you can start right away.

Everyone and anyone with an interest in our brand: bloggers, influencers, content creators, marketing agencies and more.
