### Frequently Asked Questions

Do you have online courses on Medical Qigong?

We have quite a few courses that may interest you. We don’t have a “Medical Qigong Course” however our courses are “medically integrated”. I suggest you start with 8 Trigram Qigong. You can also see all the Online Courses available.

Please note that these courses do not make you a certified teacher as we cannot personally assess your form and performance. All certified teachers are assessed personally through our Teacher trainings Events.




Can I be an apprentice to Master Tevia?

We have a standard Teacher Training Certification and apprenticeship System. We also have a downloadable curriculum for your reference. Please see our Teacher Training Certification for more details.




What is the difference between 5 Animal Summer Roads and Winter Roads?

To explain the difference of winter roads and summer roads could fill a book so I suggest to purchase the Ebook and or check the online course as it contains all the roads.

Levels 1-10 are the summer roads and levels 11-20 are the winter roads. They are sequenced, but it is not a progression of increasing difficulty which is why it does not matter which one you do first.  
  
My suggestion is to choose which date works best for you as the style of Qigong won’t be so different.




What is the difference between 5 Animals and 5 Element qigong?

The differences between 5 elements, 5 animals summer roads and 5 animals winter roads is quite a vast topic to discuss. Kindly refer to the Blog for more detailed readings. You can also refer to our Youtube channel for more.




How to find a teacher in my local area?

You can locate White Tiger Qigong Instructors in your area and check if they are available to teach.




Where is White Tiger Qigong located?

We are based in Chiang Mai, Thailand, however our Events are done worldwide.

### Health Concerns

I had an injury in the past. Is qigong safe for me?

It will depend on the severity of your injury in the past. We suggest to get a recommendation from your doctor to be safe.




I am severely obese, can I still do this course?

We will be doing at least 1.5 hours of physical practice in the morning and afternoon. If you can handle this then yes you can. Always consult your doctor if you have any doubts.




I have had mental illness, is this course suitable for me?

Qigong is not recommended for severe schizophrenia as it can further induce hallucinations. It is also not recommended for people who are currently suicidal.




I am already old, can I practice Qigong?

Qigong helps in many ways. We had students that are in their 70’s and they love our modified forms.




Does Qigong help with health concerns?

Make sure to consult a healthcare professional if you have any concerns. Qigong has helped millions of people. However, the ability of your body to heal may differ from person to person. Different forms have different effects on the body.
