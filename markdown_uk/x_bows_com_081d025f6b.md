## Points you should know about X-Bows

X-Bows employs a unique X-Bows layout that focuses on maximizing the use of modifier keys to reduce finger movement and muscle strain during typing. This design aims to alleviate ergonomic pressures and minimize stretched wrist movements, which are common issues with standard keyboards. The X-Bows Knight, for instance, is constructed with a solid monolithic aluminum alloy body, providing a sturdy and stable typing platform. Additionally, the keyboards feature highly durable key caps with a special formula on the surface to prevent them from becoming shiny over time. This attention to detail ensures a comfortable and long-lasting typing experience for users.
