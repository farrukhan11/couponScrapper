## DESCRIPTION & FEATURES

## Description

The Horizon captures the moment where the earth meets the deep blue of the infinite sky. Its captivating sunray dial is enhanced by a Moon Phase window, tracking the lunar cycle that has guided humanity for millennia.

Crafted with premium zebra wood and a sophisticated design, it is a timeless statement for the explorer and the leader who seeks new frontiers. It is a reminder that while the view changes, the vision remains, inviting you to look beyond the visible

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does shipping take?

Orders are dispatched within 1 business day even if it is personalized. Transit times are the following:  
  
**Spain:** 24–48 hours

**Europe & UK:** 2–5 business days

**Rest of the world:** 5–10 business days

### Can I adjust the watch to my wrist?

Yes. Every watch includes a free adjustment tool, allowing you to resize the bracelet in just a few minutes at home.

### Are Emporizen watches water resistant?

Our watches are splash resistant but not waterproof. They can handle everyday splashes and light rain, but we recommend avoiding swimming, showering or prolonged exposure to water.

### Can my watch be engraved?

Yes. You can add a name, a date or a meaningful message on the backplate.

Simply select the engraving option and enter your text.

### Is the engraving permanent?

Yes. Every engraving is precisely laser engraved directly onto the wood or stainless steel, creating a permanent finish that won't fade or peel over time.

### Can engraved watches be returned?

Engraved watches are personalized and cannot be returned unless they arrive damaged or have a manufacturing defect.

### Is a wooden watch a good gift?

Absolutely. Emporizen watches are a popular gift for birthdays, anniversaries, weddings, Valentine's Day, Christmas and many other special occasions.

With free personalized engraving and premium gift packaging, they make a unique and meaningful gift.

### Can I send it directly as a gift?

Yes. Every Emporizen watch arrives in a premium bamboo gift box, ready to give.

We never include pricing information inside the package, making it perfect for sending directly to your recipient.
