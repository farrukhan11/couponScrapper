# 

**IEEE SA Open is** a comprehensive open source development platform that harnesses the power of familiar open source development tools with IEEE's unparalleled member network, technical expertise and resources with a goal to accelerate innovation and enable technological advancements that positively impact the way people live, work, and communicate. IEEE SA Open aims to bridge the gap between global standards development, and open-source developer communities, accelerating the development and adoption of technical solutions aimed at benefiting society.
