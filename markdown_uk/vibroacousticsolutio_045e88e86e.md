## How Does Vibroacoustic Therapy Work?

Vibroacoustic Therapy (VAT) is the precise delivery of low-frequency, kinetic sound waves (typically 30Hz to 120Hz) directly into the body’s water content. It is a "musical massage" on a cellular level.

* **The Vagus Nerve:** Gentle vibrations stimulate the vagus nerve, instantly triggering the parasympathetic nervous system for deep relaxation.
* **Cellular Micro-Massage:** The frequencies ripple through muscle and tissue, reducing inflammation, increasing circulation, and relieving chronic pain.
* **Tactile Sound:** Unlike a sound bath you only hear, VAT is sound you *feel* throughout your entire being.
