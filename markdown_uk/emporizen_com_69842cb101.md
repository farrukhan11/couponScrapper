## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long will it take to receive my order?

Orders are dispatched within 1 business day even if it is personalized. Transit times are the following:  
  
**Spain:** 24–48 hours

**Europe & UK:** 2–5 business days

**Rest of the world:** 5–10 business days

### Can my watch be engraved?

Yes. You can add a name, a date or a meaningful message on the backplate.

Simply select the engraving option and enter your text.

### Can I adjust the watch to fit any wrist size?

Yes. Every watch includes a free adjustment tool, allowing you to resize the bracelet in just a few minutes at home.

### Are wooden watches water resistant?

Our watches are splash resistant but not waterproof. They can handle everyday splashes and light rain, but we recommend avoiding swimming, showering or prolonged exposure to water.

### Are your products made of authentic wood?

The woods used in our pieces are sourced from all around the world, and each has its own distinct set of qualities.  
  
You can see what kind of wood was used in every product page.

### Are wooden watches a good gift?

Absolutely. Emporizen watches are a popular gift for birthdays, anniversaries, weddings, Father's Day, Mother's Day, Valentine's Day, Christmas and many other special occasions.

With free personalized engraving and premium gift packaging, they make a unique and meaningful gift.
