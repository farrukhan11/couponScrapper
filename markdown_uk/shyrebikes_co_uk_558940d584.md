## Questions & answers

How do I choose the right size bike?

Measure your child’s inside leg and use our size finder — far more accurate than age alone.


What ages are Shyre Bikes suitable for?

From 18 months on the Myndie balance bike up to age 14 on our 26" bikes.


How quickly will my bike arrive?

Free UK delivery — most bikes dispatched within 2–4 working days, boxed and 90% assembled.


What warranty do you offer?

A lifetime frame warranty, plus a 90-day happiness guarantee.

Bikes arrive 90% built — just the bars, pedals and saddle. See our build guides.


Can my child try a bike before buying?

Yes — visit one of our dealers for a test ride.
