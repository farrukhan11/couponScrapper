**Will the equipment fit my existing furniture, and is it portable?**

The equipment is primarily designed and tested with a wooden frame for both stationary and portable tables. Its low-profile design allows portable tables to remain fully foldable and storable without the removal of hardware. Regarding other types of furniture, the surface must be a clear, flat surface to allow for the attachment of 3M tape. While we are currently working on new options for other furniture types, this system has not been tested or designed for furniture beyond the specified wooden-frame tables at this time.
