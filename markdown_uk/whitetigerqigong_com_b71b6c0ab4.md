## Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need prior Qigong experience to start?

No prior experience is required. Every system builds from beginner accessibility through advanced material, and each form offers seated, standing, and modified variations so complete newcomers can progress with confidence.

What is the difference between completing a course and being certified?

Completing all five levels of a system earns a Certificate of Completion. Becoming a Certified Instructor requires passing the Level 5 examination — a written theory test plus a video form analysis reviewed by senior instructors.

Is it suitable for older students or people recovering from injury?

Yes. Qigong is among the most accessible movement practices there is. Students in their seventies and eighties complete trainings, and many people returning from chronic injury find it the first practice their body can sustain.
