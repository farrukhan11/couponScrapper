### Frequently asked questions

How do you ship my order?

We ship domestically (US) through USPS, UPS, FedEx or GoFo Express depending on your location. You’ll receive a tracking number as soon as it ships.

How long does delivery take?

Most orders arrive in 3–8 business days. During holidays (like Black Friday), shipping may take 1–2 days longer.

Is this headset good for trucking noise?

Yes. Our headsets are built for truckers and cut through diesel, wind, and road noise so the person on the other end hears you clearly.

Does it work with my device?

It works with iPhone, Android, tablets, laptops, and all Bluetooth-enabled devices.

What if my headset breaks or stops working?

If it’s damaged or stops working within \*\*100 days\*\*, we’ll replace it for free—no questions asked.
You can also purchase an extended warranty for long-term protection.

Are these headsets suitable for truckers?

Yes. Over \*\*20,000+ truckers\*\* use Black&Gray headsets. Our products are built for long routes, long hours, and real road conditions.

Is the headset comfortable for long shifts?

Yes. Our headsets are designed for 10–12 hour workdays lightweight, padded, and comfortable for full-day wear.

What about warranty and support?

All headsets include a standard guarantee of 100 days.
You can also add an \*\*extended warranty\*\* for extra coverage and priority replacement.

Do you have a return or exchange policy?

Yes. You can return or exchange your headset within 100 days, even if you just changed your mind.
