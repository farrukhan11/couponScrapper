Order & Payments

Do I need an account to place an order?

No account is required to place an order. However, creating an account allows you to track orders, save favorites, and access your purchase history for added convenience.



What payment methods do you accept?

We accept Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover, JCB, Diners Club International, PayPal, Amazon Pay, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Klarna, Shop Pay, and Zip (QuadPay).



Can I get a price adjustment?

We do not offer price adjustments after a purchase. For discounts, please refer to current promotions before placing your order: Current Promotions.



Can I cancel my order?

Orders are processed or enter production shortly after purchase, cancellation requests are reviewed in accordance with our Cancellation Policy. Cancellation is not guaranteed and may be subject to applicable fees as outlined in the policy.

Shipping & Delivery

When will my order be shipped?

**Order Processing Time:**

* **Ready-To-Ship Collection (Size XS–XL):** Orders usually ship within 2–4 business days.
* **Made-To-Order Collection (Size US 2–16 and Custom Size including Bespoke Gowns):** Each piece is carefully handcrafted after purchase, which typically takes 30–45 business days. Production begins soon after your order is confirmed. Please allow a little extra time during holidays or peak seasons.

**Note:** *The shipping method you choose only affects the transit time once your order has shipped. It does not impact order processing or production time.*



When will my order be delivered?

**Estimated Delivery Time = Processing Time + Shipping Time.** Shipping options include:

* No Rush Free Shipping: 7–15+ business days
* Standard Shipping: $29 | 5–12 business days
* Express Shipping: $40 | 3–7 business days

*Delivery times may vary due to carrier delays, customs, holidays, weather, or incomplete shipping information.*



Can you expedite production or tailoring?

Yes. Optional **30-business-day Expedite Tailoring** is available for all Made-To-Order dresses, subject to limited availability. If you need your dress sooner, you may request Expedite Tailoring **after placing your order by replying to your order confirmation email or by contacting our Customer Service team**. Our team will confirm availability and provide the applicable Expedite Tailoring fee for your dress.

The **30-business-day production timeframe is an estimated timeframe and is not a guaranteed turnaround time**. It begins on the **next business day after Expedite Tailoring has been added to your order**.

The Expedite Tailoring fee is **non-refundable and final sale**. Please note that Expedite Tailoring applies to **production only** and does not include or expedite shipping. Shipping time is additional and depends on the shipping method selected for your order.



Can I change my shipping method after placing the order?

Once an order is placed, the selected shipping method cannot be changed. Please choose carefully during checkout.



Can I update my shipping address?

You may request a shipping address update **before your order is shipped** by contacting us immediately at hello@jewelclues.com. While we do our best to assist, changes cannot be guaranteed once the order has been processed for shipment.



How can I track my order shipment?

Once your order ships, you will receive a shipping confirmation email with your tracking number and a direct link to monitor your package. You can also track shipments anytime using your tracking email or our Track Your Order page.



What about customs and duties?

International order shipments may incur customs duties, taxes, or carrier handling fees determined by local authorities. These charges are the responsibility of the recipient and are not collected by JewelClues.

Design & Tailoring

What is Made-To-Order Collection and how does sizing work?

Each gown in our **Made-To-Order Collection** is individually handcrafted after your order is received. This includes both Standard Sizes (US 2–16) and Custom Sizes (Made-To-Measure). Selecting a standard size does **not** mean the dress is pre-made — only gowns clearly labeled **Ready-To-Ship (XS–XL)** are in stock and can ship immediately.

Production for Made-To-Order dresses generally takes 30–45 business days before shipment. Because each gown is handcrafted, slight variations in embroidery, print placement, or color tone are natural and make every piece uniquely yours.



How do I submit my measurements for a custom dress?

Please follow our measurement guide and submit your details using our Send Your Measurements page. Accurate measurements ensure a perfect fit for your bespoke gown.



Can I exchange or return a Made-To-Order Collection dress?

Made-To-Order collection dresses are **not eligible for exchange**. Returns may be initiated in accordance with our Return Policy. Bespoke, Personalized and Custom Size orders are final sale.



What if my dress arrives damaged or defective?

While rare, if your gown arrives damaged or defective, please contact us within 7 days of delivery. Photos or documentation may be requested for review.

Depending on the situation, we may offer store credit or, in limited cases, a replacement in the same style and variant. Replacement orders follow standard Made-To-Order production timelines.



What if my dress looks different from the website?

Each gown is handmade and exclusive. Some variations in embroidery, print placement, or color tone are natural characteristics of handcrafted production and enhance the uniqueness of your dress. No two pieces are 100% identical.



Do you offer Bespoke or Bring-Your-Own-Design (BYOD) services?

Yes! Our Atelier of Dreams welcomes bespoke requests and BYOD projects. Contact our customer care team to discuss your custom design, and we will guide you through tailoring your dream gown.

Return & Exchange

How do I return or exchange an item?

Exchanges are limited to Ready-To-Ship Collection items in a different size. Made-To-Order Collection dresses are ineligible for exchange. To exchange, initiate a return per our Return Policy and place a new order. Returns must be postmarked within the timeframe specified in the policy.



Are shipping fees refundable?

Shipping, duties, and taxes are non-refundable. Return shipping — including any insurance, duties, or other charges — is the responsibility of the buyer. Refunds apply only to the item price as outlined in our Return Policy.
