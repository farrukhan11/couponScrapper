## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the Charabanc Aroma diffuser series?

The Charabanc Aroma diffuser series grew from the Charabanc Aroma Retro Oil Diffuser, a decorative waterless essential oil diffuser for home fragrance and aromatherapy. Each design combines vintage design with modern technology, releasing a visible aromatic plume without water, heat, or an open flame.

### How do I use it?

Add pure essential oil, secure the bottle inside the diffuser, choose your mode, and let the scent build gently through the room. The simple controls help adjust aroma intensity for bedrooms, living rooms, and daily rituals.

### Is it a humidifier?

No. It is made for oil diffusion, not ultrasonic water misting, so there is no water tank and it does not add humidity to the room.

### How do I clean it?

Empty the glass bottle, add a small amount of warm water or alcohol, run the diffuser briefly, then empty and wipe it with a soft cloth before refilling with oil. For a fuller guide, visit Care & Cleaning.

### Can I use essential oils around pets?

Essential oils should be used thoughtfully around pets. Please read our Pet Safe guide for oil choices, room ventilation, and when to avoid diffusing around cats, dogs, birds, or sensitive animals.

### Can I use it while resting?

Yes. It runs quietly and includes automatic shut-off for easier everyday use during rest hours.
