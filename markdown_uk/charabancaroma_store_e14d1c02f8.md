### Why Choose a Waterless Diffuser?

**Natural Scent Release**

Spreads the scent just like nature does. Ensuring the efficacy of essential oils.

**Keep it clean**

The oil molecules stay in the air, instead of dripping or sticking to objects. No mess and avoids bacteria.

**Better Coverage**

Unlike heavy water vapor that falls quickly, the fine aroma particles travel further with air currents. Less essential oils spread the scent over a larger area.

### Cordless vs. Plug-In — what's the difference?

**Cordless vs. plug-in — choose your power style:**

* **Cordless (built-in battery)**: runs cordless for up to 10 hours per full charge, so you can move it freely between rooms. Recharge with the included USB Type-C cable.
* **Plug-In (no battery)**: runs continuously while connected to power through the included USB-A cable; it works only while plugged in.

**⚡ Power adapter:** The USB cable is included, but a wall adapter is not. Always power or charge the diffuser with a 5V/2A (10W) or higher USB adapter. A weaker adapter (e.g. 5V/1A) can cause faint mist, dim light, or slow/failed charging.
