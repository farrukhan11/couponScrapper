### Private Label Rights / Whitelabel Rights

#### What are private label rights?

**Private Label Rights (PLR)** refer to a type of licensing that allows individuals or businesses to purchase digital content—such as online courses—and modify, rebrand, and resell it as their own. PLR content is popular among entrepreneurs, marketers, and content creators who want to quickly produce and distribute content without creating it from scratch

#### What is the difference between Whitelabel and Private Label?

With Private Label Rights, you can modify the content - where as with Whitelabel, you can only sell it as your own. All courses on Uthena come with the option to purchase Private Label Rights.

#### What are Master Resell Rights?

Master Resell Rights offer you the option to also sell whitelabel rights to the courses you create after purchase. Some courses on Uthena also offer this option.

For most use-cases, Private Label Rights are recommended and sufficient.
